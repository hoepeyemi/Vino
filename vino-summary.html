Problem

Small and medium enterprises issue invoices and wait 30–90 days for payment, locking working capital they cannot redeploy. The APAC trade finance gap reached $2.5 trillion in 2022 (Asian Development Bank), driven by bank rejection rates above 40% for SME applicants.

Existing on-chain invoice protocols address the yield opportunity but not the compliance requirement. Without KYB identity verification, verified-asset settlement, and Travel Rule reporting, they are inaccessible to regulated institutions and their enterprise clients — the very buyers whose purchasing volume creates the financing demand.

Solution

vino tokenizes B2B invoices as ERC-721 NFTs on Monad — storing only a keccak256(data + salt) commitment on-chain, with no plaintext invoice data on the public ledger. Each NFT is deposited into a yield vault with three strategy tiers: Hold (0%), Conservative (3.5% APY), and Aggressive (7% APY).

An autonomous AI agent — MemoriVault — selects and executes the optimal strategy per invoice every five minutes, guided by on-chain risk scores, market regime analysis, Qwen 70B reasoning, and a three-tier hierarchical memory (L1 working context · L2 episodic RAG · L3 distilled rules). Every deposit, strategy decision, and settlement is gated by Cleanverse CVI (Verified Identity) and CVA (Verified Assets), and every cross-border transfer auto-generates a FATF Rec-16 Travel Rule report from the mint transaction hash — no manual data entry required.

CVI · CVA Integration Points
  1. 01
    generate_apass AES-CBC
    KYB registration — customerId, wallet address, chain, expiry. Returns cvRecordId displayed in the compliance audit trail as the Cleanverse proof anchor. Called at Step 1 of the onboard flow.
  2. 02
    query_apass Plain JSON
    Real-time A-Pass status check — called on every page load via useCleanverseCVI(). Returns tier, status, expiry. Drives the KYB gate on mint, deposit, and settlement; renders as the CVI banner in the nav.
  3. 03
    MockCVI.verify() On-chain · Monad
    On-chain CVI relay — the onboard API calls MockCVI.verify(wallet) via the relay wallet. The Monad txHash is stored in localStorage as cviTxHash and surfaced as a clickable Monad Explorer link in the compliance audit trail. isVerified() gates mint and deposit at the contract level.
  4. 04
    query_deposit_atoken_list AES-CBC
    Returns CVA-approved settlement tokens — USDC · USDT · EURC — with issuer, kybRequired, and per-wallet eligibility flag. Rendered with a live-vs-demo source badge. Restricts settlement to verified stablecoin assets only.
  5. 05
    verify_apass AES-CBC
    Per-wallet × per-token CVA eligibility cross-check. Called on token selection in the UI, and on every 5-minute agent analysis cycle. If a depositor's A-Pass is inactive, the agent forces a Hold strategy for that deposit until eligibility is restored. Fallback: MockCVI.isVerified() on-chain.
  6. 06
    create_payment_request AES-CBC
    Generates CVA-verified settlement instructions — invoiceNFT tokenId, CVA asset, amount, issuer address. Returns send_to address and compliance note. Source badge displayed: ✓ Cleanverse API or local format on fallback.
  7. 07
    download_travel_rule AES-CBC
    Generates the FATF Recommendation 16 Travel Rule compliance report. The mint transaction hash is auto-populated from localStorage — no manual data entry. Inline preview shows originator, beneficiary, CVA settlement asset, and compliance note. Downloadable as JSON. Mandatory for Singapore MAS PSN02 and HKMA VASP compliance.
